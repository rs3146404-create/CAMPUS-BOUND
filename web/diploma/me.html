ABOUT THIS DEPARTMENT

The Mechanical Engineering department at Campus Bound is dedicated to developing skilled professionals in the field of mechanical design, manufacturing, and engineering technology. Our curriculum provides a strong foundation in engineering principles while emphasizing practical, hands-on learning through workshop practice, laboratory experiments, machine design, and project-based activities.

Students gain knowledge in thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, manufacturing technology, machine design, automobile engineering, and industrial engineering, preparing them for diverse careers in the manufacturing and mechanical engineering industry.
