 ELECTRICIAN

The Electrician department develops students’ skills in electrical wiring, installation, and maintenance, preparing them for careers in industrial and domestic applications.

01. Our Laboratories

Electrical Machines Lab

Hands-on training in the operation, testing, and maintenance of electrical machines, including motors, generators, and transformers.

check_circle Electrical Machine Testing Equipment

Electrical Wiring & Installation Lab

Hands-on training in electrical wiring, circuit installation, and electrical safety practices for residential and industrial applications.

check_circle Electrical Wiring Tools & Equipment

Electronics Lab

Hands-on training in electronic circuits, component testing, soldering, and troubleshooting to develop practical electronics skills.
